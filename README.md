# HaproID – Free Professional Profile


**HaproID**is a **digital identity platform** that allows everyone to create their own **unique online profile**. It's not just for students, but also suitable for **job seekers, professionals, and creators.**


---

## 🌐What is HaproID?
HaproID is your **digital identity.** 
It lets you showcase your professional and personal details in a single link.


### These include:
- Your name  
- profile photo 
- Skills / Expertise  
- Social Media Links (LinkedIn, Instagram, GitHub, YouTube etc.)  
- Contact Details  
- Resume / CV (optional)  
- Projects / Portfolio  

---

## 🎯 फायदे (Benefits)

1. **One-link profile** – No need to share the link again and again.
2. **SEO-friendly** – Easily visible in Google search.  
3. **Verified & Professional** – trusted digital identity  
4. **Free & Simple** – Prepare without website or coding knowledge. 
5. **All-in-one** –For students, job seekers, creators all.
